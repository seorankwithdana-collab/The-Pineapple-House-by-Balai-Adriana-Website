Bali calm, an hour from the city.

The Pineapple House is a modern, single-storey villa wrapped in dark timber and glass, with wide eaves, a sunken pool terrace and open lawns around it. It is run by Balai Adriana as a private retreat, so the whole property is yours for your stay.

Inside, a long open living room flows into the kitchen and dining area. Outside, loungers and a shaded patio face the pool. Guests often say it feels like a resort, with the privacy of home.
